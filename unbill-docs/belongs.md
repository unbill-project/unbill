name: Belongs
desc: A structural edge that places an entry in a review neighborhood.
category:

---

An entry that belongs to another entry is reviewed in that neighborhood.
The edge is horizontal grouping for review, not vertical specialization.

Unbill uses belongs edges to group entries under front-door neighborhoods
like `applications`, `channels`, `storage`, and `frontend-ui`,
and to place those neighborhoods under the project root `unbill`.
