name: Refines
desc: A structural edge that marks an entry as a vertical specialization of another.
category:

---

An entry that refines another makes the parent entry more concrete.
The edge is vertical specialization, not horizontal grouping.

Unbill uses refines edges when an entry drills into a specific aspect
of a broader design claim, such as `fs-store` refining `unbill-storage`
or `conflict-detection` refining `bill-amendment`.
